Based on the retrieved sequences and articles, the TEST_FINAL_2 sequence is likely associated with drought tolerance in wheat (Triticum aestivum). This sequence is similar to the drought_tolerance gene JQ923471.1, which encodes a stress responsive protein (SRP) [1]. The EARLY-RESPONSIVE TO DEHYDRATION 7 (ERD7) gene, represented by accession IDs XM_044519976.1 and XM_044506807.1, is also linked to drought tolerance in wheat [2]. The practical application of this sequence in arid region agriculture in Tunisia is to improve drought tolerance in wheat varieties, enhancing their yield and resilience in water-scarce conditions.
