HG916393.1 is a cDNA clone associated with heat tolerance in wheat (Triticum aestivum). This sequence is linked to heat stress at 42°C during the post-anthesis stage, just before grain filling [2]. The gene family and specific gene name are unknown, but its expression is crucial for heat tolerance, which is essential for arid region agriculture in Tunisia. Practical applications for IRA research include using this sequence to develop heat-tolerant wheat varieties that can thrive in the challenging climate of Tunisia.
