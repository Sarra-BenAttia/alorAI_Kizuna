The sequence MZ935738.1 corresponds to a Triticum aestivum DREB transcription factor (DREB) gene, which plays a crucial role in drought tolerance by regulating the expression of stress-responsive genes [3]. This gene family is involved in stress tolerance mechanisms, such as drought, heat, and cold, by activating the expression of downstream genes that help plants to survive under adverse conditions [2]. In the context of arid region agriculture in Tunisia, this gene has significant practical applications for crop improvement, as it can be used to develop drought-tolerant wheat varieties that can thrive in water-scarce conditions [1]. The Module 1 prediction suggests that this gene is associated with drought tolerance, with a high confidence of 97.7% and a probability of 0.98.
