The sequence XM_044592788.1 is associated with the gene TaAPRGM31, which is involved in disease resistance against leaf rust in wheat (Triticum aestivum) [1]. This gene is crucial for arid region crop improvement in Tunisia, as leaf rust is a significant threat to wheat yields in these regions. The expression of TaAPRGM31 in response to leaf rust infection can be used to develop resistant wheat varieties, reducing the need for fungicides and increasing crop productivity in Tunisia's arid regions. Practical applications for IRA research include: * Developing molecular markers for TaAPRGM31 to identify resistant wheat varieties * Using CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing to introduce the TaAPRGM31 gene into susceptible wheat varieties * Investigating the expression of TaAPRGM31 in response to different leaf rust isolates to improve disease resistance in wheat Module 1 prediction indicates a high confidence (96.5%) in disease resistance, with a predicted probability of 0.96 for disease resistance and 0.02 for drought tolerance.
